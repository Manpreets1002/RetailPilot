RetailPilot

Smarter pricing for retailers. Smarter picks for shoppers. Built on public Amazon India listings, refreshed by our crawler and explained by an AI assistant that shows its evidence.

What is RetailPilot?

RetailPilot reads prices, specifications, delivery dates and customer reviews from public listings in cities across India. It turns that data into two products: a dashboard for retailers, and a shopping assistant for customers. Both answer in plain language and point to the listings behind every number.

How we can help you

For retailers

See where your price sits against competitors in your city and subcategory. Compare margins at match-lowest, hold and raise scenarios from your own landed cost. Track price trends, read what customers say about your products, and check how fresh the data is.

For customers

Tell us what you want to buy. We show the best-value picks in that category for your city today, weighing ratings, review volume and price. Then ask follow-up questions: specs, pros and cons, comparisons, and what past prices looked like.

How it works

1 · Collect

A crawler records prices, delivery dates, specs and reviews for each city, with timestamps.

2 · Analyse

Pricing, margin and sentiment tools calculate results from that data, never from guesses.

3 · Explain

The assistant answers with the numbers, the date they were observed, and what it could not find.
